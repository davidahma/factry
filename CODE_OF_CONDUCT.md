# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique the proposed change with examples, explain disagreements, and give people room to correct mistakes. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and publication of private information are grounds for moderation.

For a problem on a public thread, ask a maintainer to review it or use GitHub’s report option on the content. For private reporting to the platform, use [GitHub’s abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock discussions, and restrict participation in project spaces.
